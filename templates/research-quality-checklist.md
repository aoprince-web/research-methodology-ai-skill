# Research Quality and Ethics Checklist

Use this checklist to identify issues that need attention. The relevant criteria depend on the research approach, design, discipline, and institutional rules.

## Research logic

- [ ] The problem is specific and supported by evidence.
- [ ] The purpose, aim, objectives, and questions are mutually consistent.
- [ ] The contribution is plausible and proportionate to the study.
- [ ] The theoretical or conceptual framing is justified and used meaningfully, where applicable.
- [ ] The research approach and design fit the questions.

## Feasibility

- [ ] The target population, case, records, or dataset is defined.
- [ ] Access and recruitment routes are realistic.
- [ ] Time for approvals, recruitment, data collection, analysis, and writing is included.
- [ ] The budget, skills, software, language needs, and other resources are realistic.
- [ ] The study scope is achievable and limitations are acknowledged.

## Sampling and data collection

- [ ] The sampling or case-selection strategy is justified.
- [ ] Sample-size or adequacy rationale is stated and not presented as a guarantee.
- [ ] Instruments or protocols are suitable for the constructs and setting.
- [ ] Piloting or adaptation is planned where appropriate.
- [ ] Data sources can answer the research questions.
- [ ] Permissions, recording, transcription, and data-handling procedures are addressed.

## Analysis and claims

- [ ] Each research question has a corresponding analysis plan.
- [ ] The analysis matches the data type, design, and assumptions.
- [ ] Missing data, bias, confounding, contradictory evidence, or other relevant issues are considered.
- [ ] Integration is planned explicitly for mixed methods research.
- [ ] The intended conclusions do not exceed what the design can establish.
- [ ] Findings will be distinguished from interpretation and speculation.

## Quality and rigour

- [ ] Quality criteria fit the selected approach.
- [ ] Measurement validity, reliability, and relevant bias are considered for quantitative work.
- [ ] Trustworthiness, reflexivity, and context are considered for qualitative work.
- [ ] Both strands and their integration are considered for mixed methods work.
- [ ] Research decisions and changes will be documented.

## Ethics and data management

- [ ] Consent is informed and voluntary.
- [ ] Risks, discomfort, benefits, and participant burden are considered.
- [ ] Privacy, confidentiality, and identifiability are addressed.
- [ ] Data storage, access, retention, and destruction are planned.
- [ ] Gatekeeper permissions and institutional ethics review are addressed.
- [ ] Approval status is accurately described.
- [ ] Power differences, vulnerability, language, and cultural context are considered.
- [ ] Data and quotations will not be fabricated or misrepresented.

## Reporting and sources

- [ ] The required structure, word limit, spelling convention, and citation style are followed.
- [ ] Important claims are supported by suitable sources.
- [ ] References and DOIs have been verified where possible.
- [ ] Limitations are reported honestly.
- [ ] The report does not claim representativeness, causation, saturation, or approval without a defensible basis.
