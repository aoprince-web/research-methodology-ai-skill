## How to review the matrix

1. **Traceability:** Can each question be traced to the stated problem and purpose?
2. **Evidence:** Is the proposed data capable of answering the question?
3. **Selection:** Is there a defensible reason for choosing these participants, cases, documents, or records?
4. **Method fit:** Does the collection method produce the required evidence?
5. **Analysis fit:** Is the proposed analysis suitable for the question and data type?
6. **Inference:** Does the intended conclusion stay within the limits of the design?
7. **Feasibility:** Can the researcher complete the plan with available time, access, skills, and resources?
8. **Ethics:** Are consent, privacy, risk, and permissions addressed where relevant?

## Red flags to resolve

- An objective has no corresponding question.
- A question has no identified data source.
- The proposed instrument does not measure the stated concept.
- A cross-sectional association is described as proof of causation.
- A qualitative study claims statistical representativeness without a suitable basis.
- A mixed methods study collects two data types but does not explain integration.
- The sample or dataset is chosen for convenience but the limitations are not acknowledged.
- The proposed conclusion is stronger than the evidence can support.
