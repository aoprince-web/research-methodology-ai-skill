## 12. Findings narrative plan

For each theme:
1. State the analytic claim.
2. Explain the pattern and its boundaries.
3. Present a carefully selected quotation with participant label.
4. Interpret the quotation in context.
5. Consider variation or contradictory evidence.
6. Link the interpretation to the relevant research question.
7. Avoid adding literature discussion unless the requested section requires it.
